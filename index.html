About the game

Every level in Thinkora is a trick. The question looks easy, but the obvious answer is usually wrong. Read carefully, think differently, and tap the answer that is really right.

What you’ll play

  • 1,000 levels that get harder as you go
  • Trick riddles, logic and number puzzles
  • Memory challenges and quick-fire rounds against the clock
  • Tap, hold and catch-the-button challenges
  • Earn up to 3 stars per level and use coins for hints
  • Play offline, anywhere
